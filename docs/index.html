What this workspace does — and what it will not do

Am I Scholar is an offline-first research and study workspace for the browser and Android. Core notes stay on this device. There is no account and no cloud sync.

  • Research papers. A question, discipline, and citation style, then twelve modules: Topic Lab, Literature Radar, Evidence Matrix, Outline Architect, Draft Studio, Style Coach, Citation Center, Originality and Integrity, Review Simulator, Submission Kit, Revision and Response, and Archive and Reproducibility.
  • Literature Radar. When you search, this browser asks Crossref, OpenAlex, and PubMed. Results are metadata, merged when the DOI matches, and saved as unverified until you read the original. The Android app can also query arXiv and DOAJ.
  • Citation Center. Offline CSL drafts for APA, MLA, Chicago, IEEE, Harvard, and Vancouver, plus CSL-JSON, BibTeX, and RIS. A local PDF reader can keep highlights and notes. Check the journal’s instructions before you submit.
  • Draft Studio. You write the manuscript. Export Markdown, HTML, or print. An optional prompt copies only the sources you select. This app does not call an AI provider.
  • Student workspace. Courses, tasks, notes, flashcards, a focus timer, calendar import and export, and local lecture recordings. Transcription is manual. Recordings are not uploaded and are left out of JSON backups.
  • Backups. Plain JSON, an Android project file, or a passphrase-encrypted full backup. The passphrase cannot be recovered if you lose it.

The Review Simulator is a checklist you complete, not a reviewer. You remain the author.

Backup and data portability

Browser storage can be cleared. Save a backup outside this device regularly. Restoring a full web backup replaces this browser's current data after confirmation.

Encrypted full backups include web research, portable Android projects, and student workspace data; they require a passphrase of at least 12 characters. Keep it separately: it cannot be recovered. Android-format exports contain one paper, its notes and sources. The Android app's project-restore feature can import these exports. The web can also import Android-format backups as new projects.

Privacy and capabilities

No accounts, paid APIs, ads, or cloud sync. Literature Radar sends search terms to public metadata providers only when you search. An optional contact email is sent to providers that request it and to Unpaywall for Open Access lookups. Search results are transient until imported; saved research data stays in this browser. Citation output is a draft and must be checked against the target journal.
