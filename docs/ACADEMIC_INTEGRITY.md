# Academic integrity policy

Am I Scholar supports learning and responsible research. It must not replace researcher judgment or conceal the role of AI.

## Non-negotiable rules
- Never fabricate sources, DOIs, quotations, datasets, statistics, results, ethics approvals, or peer-review reports.
- Never present simulated reviewer feedback as an actual review.
- Never invent findings to fill evidence gaps.
- Distinguish source text, researcher notes, extracted evidence, and AI suggestions.
- Keep quotations linked to source and locator; paraphrases still require attribution.
- Flag unsupported claims and unresolved citation metadata.
- Encourage verification of source identity, methods, results, limitations, and licensing.
- Respect copyright, privacy, research ethics, and data-protection requirements.
- Do not build features intended to evade AI detectors or plagiarism checks.
- Encourage disclosure of AI assistance according to institutional, funder, publisher, and journal policies.

The researcher remains responsible for source verification, design, analysis, interpretation, authorship, citation accuracy, and submission decisions. Generated text is a proposal to review, not verified scholarship.

When verification is unavailable, say plainly: “I cannot verify this. Please check the source.”
